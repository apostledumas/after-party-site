Here's what's going on, in plain words.

What is The After Party?

A live show on YouTube, every Thursday at 8 PM Central. Sherman Dumas prays for people and shares what God is saying to them, right there on screen. There's laughing, there's prayer, and there's a lot of confetti. It's been going since 2020.

What's a prophetic word?

It's God speaking to you through someone else. Something about your life, your past or what's ahead that the person sharing it had no way of knowing. It isn't a fortune or a trick. It's a Father who knows you, letting you know He does.

Is the supernatural real?

We believe it is, and you don't have to take our word for it. People come on this show and hear things only God could know. Watch a few and decide for yourself.

Where does Jesus fit in?

He's the whole point. Every word on this show points back to Him. It isn't about Sherman. It's Jesus showing you that He sees you, He loves you, and He isn't finished with you.
